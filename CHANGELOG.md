# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Character:
  - Titles: pick the title you wear from the side panel.
  - Gear sets: save, wear, rename, re-icon and delete equipment sets; drag one to your action bars. A set asked for in combat goes on when the fight ends.
  - Gear slots show their sockets and gems (shift-right-click to socket), stack counts, cooldowns, and locked or broken items; hold Alt over a slot to wear another item for it, put it in your bags, or leave it out of a gear set.
  - Reputation: click a faction for its details, with At War, Inactive and Show as Experience Bar.
  - Currency: click a currency for its details, with Unused and Show on Backpack; shift-click shows it on the backpack, or links it while you type in chat.
  - Honor: this week's cap, your season progress, the next rank's rewards and the season's countdown.
  - Pet: your pet's level, loyalty, happiness, experience and stats, with its model.
  - Skills: click a skill for its details, with a weapon's odds against your level and a raid boss.
- Social: friends in Polestar's look (Battle.net friends marked, favorites first), with the game's own tooltip (character, note, tags, broadcast, last online); friend requests on their own page with Accept and Decline, Report player and Block invites; recent allies grouped Pinned and Recent with a pin you click to pin or unpin, their note and the game's filter; the full ignore list with blocked Battle.net invites and Ignore player; and the raid as the game shows it: drag players between groups, click to target, right-click for the raid menu, leader and main tank marks, ready check, All assist, role counts, Raid info with Extend, and party conversion. Click whispers, double-click invites, right-click opens the game's menu (set favorites and notes there); Add friend works as the game's (target, BattleTag or name); the status menu sets your Battle.net broadcast. Quick Join and Recruit a Friend stay in the game's window where the game has them (the chat's friends button).
- Bank: the banker's Bank and Warband bank in Polestar's look: every bag's slots in one grid, the bag slots to put bags in and buy, quest marks and cooldowns, search, Clean up, Deposit all, money in and out, a warning before a refundable item goes into the Warband bank, a locked bank that says why, and right-click to your bags.
- Mail: the mailbox in Polestar's look: unread and read letters, each with its tooltip; a letter beside it with its copy, money and items, auction invoices, Take, Reply, Return and Delete; shift-click to take a letter whole; Open all; more mail fetched past 50; cash on delivery that asks first; and a send page with attachments (from the bags' right-click too), money and COD, Send off until To and Subject are filled, and a warning before mailing a refundable item.
- Gossip: talking to an NPC opens Polestar's window: the NPC's portrait, a friendship NPC's standing, the greeting, quests to take and hand in, and the options with their icons; keys 1-9 pick. A vendor with one option opens straight away, as before.
- Merchant: the vendor in Polestar's look: Buy and Buyback, prices in red when you can't afford them, click to buy one, right-click a stack, shift-click a count; every currency and item cost with its tooltip, the vendor's currencies you hold, and the game's warnings before costly or non-refundable buys; the last item sold to buy back; Repair all, Guild repair, Repair an item, Sell junk, a refund that asks first, and Polestar's Restock with its total.
- Trainer: the trainer in Polestar's look: categories with what you can learn, each spell's cost and needs (what you lack in red), Categorize in the filter, your money or your pet's training points, a profession's rank and next rank, and Train. Profession trainers ask before you learn.
- Quest dialog: quest givers open Polestar's window: the offer with its objectives and rewards, progress with what you still need, and the reward choice with Polestar's best-pick marks; the quest giver's portrait beside it. Quests an item or an area starts go to the objective tracker, as in the game's window.
- Loot: the loot window in Polestar's look, at the cursor when the game's option is on, titled Fishing when fishing, where Edit Mode put it; quest items marked, master loot's menu. Roll windows and loot confirmations stay the game's.
- Auction House: the auctioneer in Polestar's look: search and categories, commodity buying with a quote that asks again if the price rises, item buyout and bids, posting with duration and deposit, and your auctions with cancel. Now with everything the stock window has: the full category tree, the search options (level range, usable, upgrades, rarity), sortable columns, favorites, the Bids tab with outbid marks, the quote's countdown, a starting price and Buyout only when posting, the current listings while selling, multisell progress, right-click from the bags to sell, your auctions and bids by item, the stock tooltips and links, and the WoW Token where the game shows it.
- World Map: a filter button and a map pin button at the map's top right, as the game's map has them: the filters work with the quest log shut, and the pin places a waypoint you can track.
- World Map: your coordinates and the cursor's at the map's bottom left, following the game's map coordinate options.
- Quest log: right-click a quest to track, focus (point your guide to it), share or abandon it, or a header to track or untrack all its quests; the focused quest is marked, and a quest's tooltip lists its objectives. Quests show party members on them and the money they still need, campaign headers always show, and the count turns red when the log is over full.
- Spellbook: a Filter button beside the search with the game's Hide passives, Use flyouts and Show all ranks; flyouts such as portals as one entry that lists its spells; the game's skill-line icons on the categories; spells on no action bar marked; the assisted combat spell under General where the game has one.
- Talents: Activate on the spec you are not using, Reset (staged until you apply it), the game's search options Hide passives and Show ranks, learnt talents drag to your bars, and disabled buttons say why.
- Professions: favorite recipes (a star on the recipe, a right-click menu, Favorites first), Track recipe, Link profession and posting it to a channel from Options, Clear filters, item-level search (~20 or 10-20), skill-up chance in a recipe's tooltip, View crafters for the guild's recipes, the profession's tool and gear slots where the game has them, and Craft says why it is disabled.

## 0.0.5

- Chat: each chat window is one Polestar window, its tabs as the header and the input line as the footer. The side buttons, scroll bar, resize grip, minimised windows, the dock's overflow list, the chat's right-click menus and chat bubbles (outside instances) take Polestar's look too.
- Tooltips: the comparison tooltips' "Equipped" plate in Polestar's look; tooltips a little more see-through than the windows, following the Background opacity.
- World Map: Polestar draws its map features on our map, when the Polestar version installed supports it.
- Releases upload to CurseForge.

## 0.0.4

- World Map: the game's map in Polestar's look, opened from the map key. The sidebar lists the maps above, the zones, a zone's dungeons and caves, and a map's floors; zoom steps and full screen.
- Quest log: a flyout beside the map, from the quest log key and micro button (shift-click for the game's map). Headers that fold, search, each quest's level (elite marked +), difficulty colour and objectives, and the map's filter menu, kept in step with the game's map.
- Quest details: click a quest for its objectives, text and rewards (experience with its share of your level), with Track, Share and Abandon.
- Tooltips: the game's own tooltips in Polestar's look, with every line other addons add to them.
- Chat: the game's own chat windows, tabs and input line in Polestar's look, at your chat opacity.
- Switch a window between Polestar's and the game's at once: "Use game window" in its "..." menu, the Polestar button above the game's window, or `/pui <window> on|off` (crafting after `/reload`). `/pui tooltips` and `/pui chat` switch the same way.
- The windows dock from the top left as the game's own do (`/pui dock off` frees them) and follow Polestar's Menu scale, its slider in each header.
- Crafting: the window is titled by its profession.
- Known: map and quest links, the quest tracker and the game's other ways to the map still open the game's map.

## 0.0.3

- Legacy: Forever's Legacy System window in Polestar's look, opened from its key, the achievements key and the Legacy micro button (shift-click for the game's own). Rewards, Challenges and the Tree on a rail on the left.
- Reward Track: each reward at the legacy points it needs, the next one marked, with progress toward it.
- Challenges: categories that fold as the game's do, search, and the game's Completed/Incomplete filters; each challenge's legacy points and the date earned; click for its objectives, shift-click to link it in chat or track it.
- Legacy Tree: the Professions, Adventure and Progression trees; click to learn a rank, right-click to take one back, then Apply, Undo or Reset.
- Crafting: an enchant gets an item slot, listing what it can go on (worn items marked); long recipe names take a second line.
- Known: challenge links in chat still open the game's window.

## 0.0.2

- Professions: a card per profession with its rank, its spells (click to open, drag to your bars) and the game's card art; unlearn a primary profession from its card.
- Crafting: the game's crafting window in Polestar's look. Recipes by category, "Can make now" and "Recent" first, search and the game's own filters; the product, its reagents and what else it needs; Craft and Craft all with a quantity.
- Switch between professions from the rail on the left; both windows open in one shared place.
- A spell or item dragged from our windows now drops where you release it, and a fast drag takes the one you pressed.
- Spellbook cooldowns show in combat, and the micro buttons no longer cause blocked-action errors in combat.
- Known: casting by click from our windows (spells, professions) works out of combat; dragging works in combat. Other addons' panels in the game's crafting window (Auctionator, CraftProfit, CraftShuffle) do not show in ours yet: `/pui crafting off` and `/reload` give the game's window back.
