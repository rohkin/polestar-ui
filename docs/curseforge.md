# Polestar: UI

Polestar: UI remakes the game's own windows in the look of [Polestar](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/polestar), drawn with Polestar's own widgets, so they follow its scheme, accent and font live. **It needs Polestar installed**, and it is built for **WoW Forever**.

## Windows it replaces or restyles

- **Character**: gear around the model, a stats drawer, gear sets, titles, Reputation, Currency, PvP, Skills and Statistics.
- **Spellbook**: every tab, search, cooldowns and pet autocast.
- **Talents**: the trees on the class's art, with points staged until you Apply.
- **Professions and Crafting**: a card per profession, and recipes with filters, reagents and Craft all.
- **Legacy**: rewards, challenges and the legacy tree.
- **World Map and Quest Log**: the game's map with a quest log beside it.
- **Social**: friends, ignore list and raid.
- **Bank, Mail, Auction House**: bank and Warband bank in one grid, the mailbox with Open all, the full auctioneer.
- **Gossip, Quest dialog, Merchant, Trainer, Loot**: NPC and loot windows in the same look.
- **Tooltips and Chat**: the game's own, restyled, with every line other addons add.

## Switching back to the game's window

- Open the window's "..." menu and pick **Use game window**.
- Or type `/pui <window> off` (and `/pui <window> on` to return). `/pui` lists every window and its state.
- `/pui dock off` lets you drag windows anywhere instead of docking them from the top left.

You can't switch while in combat. Crafting changes after `/reload`.

## Known limits

- Map and quest links in chat, the quest tracker and the game's other routes to the map still open the game's own map.
- Challenge links in chat still open the game's Legacy window.
- With Forever's gamepad UI on, everything starts off and `/pui` isn't available.
- Still early: more windows are on the way.
